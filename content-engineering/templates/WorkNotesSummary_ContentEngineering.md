# WorkNotesSummary — Content Engineering

## PURPOSE
Guides the AI in producing a concise, structured summary of a Content Engineering incident from cleaned work notes.

---

## SUMMARY STRUCTURE

Produce a summary with these four sections. Each section should be 1–3 sentences. Use plain language — avoid jargon unless it is a product name.

**Problem:**
What the user reported. What was not working, and for whom.

**Environment:**
Which platform, tool, or system was affected (e.g., AEM authoring, DAM, search index, TMS). Include locale or region if relevant.

**Actions Taken:**
What was investigated or changed during resolution. Include who acted (content team, CMS admin, vendor support) if mentioned.

**Resolution:**
How the issue was resolved, or if it is unresolved, what the current state is.

---

## TONE AND LENGTH GUIDELINES

- Write in past tense (the incident is closed).
- Keep the total summary under 120 words.
- Do not include personal names or email addresses.
- Do not speculate about root cause — describe only what is stated in the work notes.

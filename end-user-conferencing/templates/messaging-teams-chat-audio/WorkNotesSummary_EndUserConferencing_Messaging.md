# WorkNotesSummary — End User Conferencing Messaging

## CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS – READ FIRST

Guide the AI in producing a concise, structured summary of a **Messaging - Teams Chat & Audio** incident from cleaned work notes. The summary should be readable in less than a minute and should focus on the outcome.

## PURPOSE
Guide the AI in producing a concise, structured summary of a Messaging incident from cleaned work notes.

---

## SUMMARY STRUCTURE

Produce a summary with these four sections. Each section should be 1-3 sentences. Use plain language; avoid jargon unless it is a product name.

**Problem:**
What the user reported. What was not working, and for whom.

**Environment:**
Which Teams surface was affected, such as desktop, web, Outlook add-in, call audio, presence, or call log.

**Actions Taken:**
What was investigated or changed during resolution.

**Resolution:**
How the issue was resolved, or if it is unresolved, what the current state is.

---

## TONE AND LENGTH GUIDELINES

- Write in past tense.
- Keep the total summary under 120 words.
- Do not include personal names or email addresses.
- Do not speculate about root cause.

## MESSAGING SUMMARY EMPHASIS
- Make the history/sync symptom obvious when chat history is missing or not reflecting.
- If the issue is send/receive related, state that clearly in the Problem and Resolution sections.
- If the issue is presence-related, say whether the status was stuck, incorrect, or could not be cleared.
- If the issue is audio-related, name whether the microphone, speaker, or call hearing was affected.

## CRITICAL OUTPUT RULES

- Keep the summary under 120 words.
- Use past tense.
- Do not include personal names or email addresses.

# WorkNotesCleanup — End User Conferencing Messaging

## CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS – READ FIRST

Clean up noisy work notes for the **Messaging - Teams Chat & Audio** service offering. Keep only the meaningful troubleshooting content that helps explain the user-visible symptom, the technical cause, and the final fix.

## PURPOSE
Define cleanup rules for noisy work notes.

## RULES
- Remove repeated status updates that do not change the outcome.
- Keep the final resolution, the user-facing symptom, and the Teams-specific details.
- Preserve evidence that explains why the incident was categorized.
- Keep exact phrases such as missing chats, not reflecting, not loading, cannot send, cannot hear, out of office, or administrator disabled chat.
- Prefer the final technical root cause and final fix over intermediate troubleshooting noise.
- Retain device, app, and version clues when they explain why the issue happened.
- Drop unrelated troubleshooting chatter.

## MESSAGING CLEANUP PRIORITY
1. Keep the shortest statement of the user symptom.
2. Keep the agent-confirmed fix or disposition.
3. Keep the evidence that shows whether the issue was sync, policy, client, audio, or login related.
4. Remove duplicate restatements of the same missing-chat complaint.

## CRITICAL OUTPUT RULES

- Keep the notes chronological if timestamps exist.
- Remove duplicates and system chatter only.
- Preserve the exact technical evidence that supports the final classification.

## WorkNotesSummary — Messaging - Teams Chat and Audio

### PURPOSE
Create a concise, outcome-focused summary from cleaned work notes.

### REQUIRED STRUCTURE
**Problem:** State the Teams chat, message, audio, status, notification, external-access, voicemail, or related symptom and its user impact.

**Environment:** Identify the affected Teams surface and relevant client/device context, including desktop/web/mobile comparison when documented.

**Actions Taken:** Summarize meaningful diagnostics and corrective actions in chronological order.

**Resolution:** State the verified outcome, recurrence, workaround, escalation, or unresolved status.

### WRITING RULES
- Maximum 120 words.
- Use past tense and plain language.
- Do not include personal names, phone numbers, user IDs, or email addresses.
- Retain relevant incident, problem, major-incident, Microsoft tracking, KB, version, and error identifiers.
- Distinguish missing history from deleted/retained content.
- Distinguish temporary recovery from confirmed permanent resolution.
- Distinguish hardware, driver, device selection, permissions, connectivity, service-side, and policy causes.
- Do not infer cache corruption because cache clearing was performed.
- Do not say resolved unless the ticket evidence supports it.

### MISSING INFORMATION
- Missing environment: The affected client or device context was not documented.
- Missing action: No meaningful troubleshooting action was documented.
- Missing outcome: The final outcome was not documented.

### OUTPUT RULES
Use exactly the four section labels above. Do not add taxonomy fields.
